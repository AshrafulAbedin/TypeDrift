The India versus Australia World Test Championship final at The Oval in London showcased one of the fiercest and most enduring rivalries in international cricket, with both teams bringing their absolute best to the biggest stage the longest format of the game has to offer. Australia's disciplined bowling attack, led by experienced campaigners who exploited the English conditions expertly with their seam and swing bowling, put India under relentless pressure from the very first session and never allowed them to settle into any kind of comfortable rhythm. Travis Head's magnificent century in the first innings was a match-defining knock that shifted the momentum decisively in Australia's favor, combining aggressive stroke play with intelligent shot selection that frustrated India's bowlers throughout his memorable time at the crease. India's batting lineup, despite containing some of the finest players in all of world cricket, struggled badly to build meaningful partnerships and convert promising starts into the big scores that were needed to compete with Australia's imposing total. Virat Kohli, who had carried such enormous expectations from billions of passionate fans back home, was unable to produce the defining innings that the grand occasion demanded of him. The result underscored Australia's remarkable consistency in ICC events and raised serious questions about India's approach to overseas conditions and high-pressure matches.
