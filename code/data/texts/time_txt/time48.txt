My Hero Academia concluded its long run as one of the defining superhero manga of its generation, bringing Izuku Midoriya's journey from a quirkless boy to the greatest hero to a close that satisfied many fans while leaving others wanting more from Kohei Horikoshi's vibrant world. The final war arc pushed every character to their absolute limits, with battles against Shigaraki and All For One testing not just the physical strength of the heroes but their fundamental beliefs about what it means to save people and whether everyone, even the most broken villains, deserves a chance at redemption. Deku's final battle required him to draw upon every quirk he had inherited through One For All, combining the powers of all previous holders in sequences that showcased Horikoshi's incredible talent for dynamic action choreography and emotional storytelling simultaneously. Bakugo's character development from an arrogant bully to a genuinely heroic figure willing to sacrifice everything for his friends was one of the most satisfying arcs in the entire series, his relationship with Deku evolving from toxic rivalry to mutual respect and deep friendship. Todoroki's confrontation with his father Endeavor's legacy and his brother Dabi provided some of the most emotionally intense moments in the manga. The series left an enduring impact on the superhero genre in manga and anime.
