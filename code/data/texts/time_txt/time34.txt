Sakamoto Days surprised manga readers worldwide with its unique blend of retired-assassin comedy and explosive action sequences that set it apart from everything else being published in Weekly Shonen Jump magazine. Taro Sakamoto, once the most feared and deadly assassin in the entire underworld, retired after falling in love with a convenience store clerk and now lives a peaceful life running a small family shop with his wife and young daughter in quiet suburban bliss. However, his deadly past constantly catches up with him as former rivals, old enemies, and assassination organizations seek him out, forcing this now overweight and seemingly harmless shopkeeper to unleash the lethal skills he never actually lost despite years of quiet domestic life away from violence. The comedy comes from the absurd contrast between Sakamoto's mundane daily existence of managing inventory and serving customers and the incredible violence he is capable of when someone threatens his peaceful life and beloved family. His partner Shin, a psychic who can read minds and serves as a reluctant assistant in the convenience store, adds another layer of humor and provides excellent action sequences of his own during the more intense arcs. The series manages to balance genuinely thrilling combat with heartwarming family moments and laugh-out-loud comedy perfectly.
