In One Piece, the revelation of Luffy's true Devil Fruit being the mythical Hito Hito no Mi Model Nika changed the entire landscape of a series that had already been running for more than twenty-five years and over one thousand chapters of storytelling. Fans who had followed Monkey D. Luffy's incredible journey from the East Blue to Wano Country were stunned as decades of mystery about the Gomu Gomu no Mi unraveled in a single extraordinary chapter that redefined everything they thought they knew about their beloved main character. Gear Fifth transformed Luffy into a figure of pure imagination, his rubber body now limited only by his creativity rather than any conventional laws of physics or established power systems. The battle against Kaido in Wano was an epic confrontation that drew on years of careful buildup, strategic alliances, and deep character development to deliver a climax truly worthy of one of the longest-running stories in all of manga history. Oda's genius lay not just in the twist itself, but in how perfectly it fit with everything that had come before, recontextualizing Luffy's powers, the World Government's obsession, and Shanks's decision to entrust the fruit to a young boy from Foosha Village all those years ago. Liberation and joy remain at the heart of One Piece.
