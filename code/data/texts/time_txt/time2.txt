In the 2022 FIFA World Cup final held in Lusail, Qatar, Lionel Messi and Kylian Mbappe delivered what many consider the greatest football match in history. Argentina took an early lead through Messi's penalty and Angel Di Maria's brilliant team goal, seemingly cruising to victory with a two goal advantage. But Mbappe had other plans entirely, scoring twice in ninety-seven seconds to force extra time in one of the most dramatic comebacks ever witnessed on the world stage. Messi scored again in extra time, only for Mbappe to complete his hat-trick from the penalty spot, making it three goals apiece. The tension during the penalty shootout was unbearable for billions watching around the globe. Emiliano Martinez's heroic saves helped Argentina clinch the trophy, and Messi finally lifted the one prize that had eluded him throughout his legendary career. The images of Messi draped in a bisht, holding the golden trophy aloft, became iconic photographs that will be remembered for decades to come. Mbappe, despite losing, proved he is the future of football with a performance that defied belief. This single match encapsulated everything beautiful about the beautiful game and reminded the world why football matters so much.
