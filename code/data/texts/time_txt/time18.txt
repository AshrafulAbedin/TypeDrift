The two sixes off Haris Rauf in the penultimate over, struck with timing and raw power that seemed to bend the laws of physics themselves, turned what appeared to be a certain defeat into a moment of pure sporting ecstasy for billions of fans watching around the world. Kohli's emotional celebration after hitting the winning runs, the sheer relief and joy visible on his face after years of criticism about his form in major tournaments, was a powerful reminder that even the greatest athletes carry doubts and fears. The innings was not just about brute force or natural talent, but about the mental resilience and competitive spirit that separates truly elite athletes from the merely excellent.
